# Slideclick

Turn your Android device into a Bluetooth slide clicker.

Slideclick acts as a standard Bluetooth HID keyboard to control your presentation slides out of the box. No companion apps or drivers required.

## Requirements

- Android 9.0 or higher
- A host computer with Bluetooth support

## Installation

You can download the APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/sinatoe/slideclick/releases) section.

## Usage

1. **Pair your device:** Go to your computer's standard Bluetooth settings and pair your Android phone.
2. **Open and control:** Launch Slideclick, grant the requested permissions, and use the on-screen arrows to navigate your presentation.
